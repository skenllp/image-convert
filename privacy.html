Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Image processing

ImageConvert converts, compresses and resizes images using your browser's Canvas API. For these core features, your image files are never uploaded to our servers — all processing happens locally on your device.

What we don't collect

We don't require an account, and we don't store the images you convert. Files exist only in your browser's memory for the duration of your session.
